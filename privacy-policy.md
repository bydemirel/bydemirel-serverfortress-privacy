Privacy Policy
----------------

Last updated: October 29, 2026

### Introduction
This Privacy Policy explains how Server Fortress: Tower Defense ("Server Fortress", "the app") handles information. Server Fortress was built as a free app, with no ads and no in-app purchases. The SERVICE is provided at no cost and is intended for use as is.

In short: **Server Fortress does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal information.**

### Information Collection and Use
The app does not ask for, collect or transmit any personally identifiable information. No account is needed, and the app never asks for your name, email address, phone number, contacts or photos.

Your game progress (levels won, stars, certificates, records) and your settings (language, sound, theme) are stored only on your own device, using the device's local app storage. This data never leaves your device. Deleting the app deletes this data.

### Cookies
The app does not use cookies.

### Location Information
The app does not access or use your location.

### Device Information
The app does not collect device identifiers or device information.

### Service Providers
The app does not use any third-party services that collect information: there is no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting. The app works fully offline and does not connect to the internet.

### Security
Because no personal information is collected or transmitted, none is held by us. Data stored on your device is protected by your device's own security.

### Children's Privacy
This Service is intended for users of all ages, including children. The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

### Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page and are effective immediately after they are posted.
